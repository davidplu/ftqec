# Extraction circuit-distance contract

The task is nondemolition measurement of an independent stabilizer generating basis, in every declared extraction round. Signed Stim measurement flows and logical-preservation flows check the ideal instrument. Empty or incomplete extraction cannot pass. General CSS/non-CSS codes use binary symplectic operators; supplied algebraic distance is not trusted.

The distance criterion is zero declared extraction-detector effects AND zero ideal final stabilizer syndrome, with nonzero logical Pauli coset (or declared observable effect). Initial data code space and final stabilizer syndrome are ideal boundaries. This is an extraction detector-model distance, not algebraic code distance, complete physical-memory distance, or arbitrary-input logical protection.

Every nonidentity Pauli alternative after each physical one-/two-qubit unitary, reset or explicit idle is retained. Measurement locations admit classical flips. A correlated two-qubit Pauli is one fault, never split into independently charged edges. At most one alternative may be selected at a physical operation. There are no extra implicit idle faults, matching the preserved model.

A verified upper witness below t makes the candidate INVALID; a weight-t witness does not invalidate target t. Lower-bound milestones keep the requested target unchanged. UNKNOWN retains unresolved weight intervals.

Each proof binds the full normalized circuit, scientific source, fault model and full physical inventory. Graph projection drops detector constraints only as a conservative relaxation. Each logical coordinate is covered separately, and every graph root is checked. Every positive uses an independently regenerated forward inventory and lower-proof checking. Every negative replays the simultaneous legal physical faults. A matching legal upper witness establishes exact distance only when a full lower bound also matches.
