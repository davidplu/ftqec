# General extraction-distance certification

FTQEC 0.1.0 retains graph potentials, full-signature location-exclusive MITM, physical-location DP, SAT/CNF, and separator elimination. The exact physical fault universe is unchanged: operation-local Pauli alternatives, readout flips, ideal code-space syndrome boundaries, and at most one alternative per physical location. An extraction lower bound excludes all harmful undetected legal sets below the requested target. It is not an arbitrary-input physical-memory guarantee.

The new `decomposition` backend first applies independently checked whole-location domain reduction and then suffix-span separator DP. `decomposition_span` exposes span pruning alone, and `decomposition_legacy` preserves the previous separator algorithm for matched experiments. Graph eligibility is tested against every configured projection for every logical channel. A raw hyperedge alone is not an ineligibility proof.

For each physical location, let its finite detector/logical domain include identity. A location may map to a retained original location only if every effect belongs to that retained domain and the retained domain is closed under XOR. All faults mapped to one retained location can then be combined into identity or one legal alternative there, with identical total detector/logical parity and no greater weight. Conversely, every reduced legal assignment is a legal original assignment. This proves equivalence of the existence of a harmful assignment below a target. Nonclosed domains cannot absorb other locations. Null-effect locations may be omitted only when all their detector/logical effects are zero.

The verifier reconstructs the original inventory using forward physical propagation, checks complete location coverage, domain inclusion, XOR closure, and retained-inventory identity, then checks every separator transition using a distinct recurrence. Full correlated alternatives are retained at every retained location. This does not assert equality of final data Paulis, only the detector and logical effects relevant to the declared exclusion property; harmful witnesses are always physically replayed.

Suffix-span pruning excludes a partial detector pattern only if it lies outside the linear span of every remaining physical alternative. This span is an over-approximation: membership is not a validity claim, and no legal completion is removed. The independent checker uses low-pivot elimination rather than the producer's high-pivot implementation. Minimum-weight states are merged only for identical detector and logical parity at the same processed-location boundary.

All state, work, CPU, and memory safeguards retain UNKNOWN with completed bounds. Statistical estimates cannot promote UNKNOWN to CERTIFIED. The portfolio remains deterministic; broad empirical superiority of a learned or adaptive ranking policy is not established by this study.

Domain reduction is only an exclusion-proof equivalence. It does not preserve fault probabilities or multiplicities and is not used to simplify the Monte Carlo or decoder noise model. All logical-error measurements use the original complete physical circuit and its declared operation noise.
