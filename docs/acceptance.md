## Scientific validation and unresolved requirements

The final regression suite covers optimized/forward effects, Boolean fault replay, signed Stim semantics, positive and negative proof checks, malformed code/logicals, physical identity, cache corruption, resume, real subprocess concurrency and memory stopping. Lint and configured type checking pass. CI is configured locally; hosted CI has not run. Large migrated untyped functions are not thereby strictly typed.

The comparison uses matched inputs, declared noise and seeds; its four policy labels describe this new implementation. The `witness` policy is not a runtime-matched execution of the entire historical optimizer. Therefore a comparison against the pre-existing optimizer remains NOT_RUN. Extra-shot policies spend more measured compute; no equal-cost superiority is claimed.

The target2 development pool supplies independently positive controls and a limited false-rejection check. It cannot establish target3 false-rejection safety, true resource–LER frontier retention, family-level generalization, or large-code efficiency. Monte Carlo confidence bounds are nominal per evaluation, not simultaneous or optional-stopping guarantees.

Certificates concern extraction with ideal syndrome boundaries. Arbitrary-input protection, full physical memory and higher-distance feasibility remain outside demonstrated scope. Hardware modules stay outside the new core. General non-CSS input is implemented; no subsystem, bosonic or non-stabilizer support is claimed.

Remaining work includes stabilizer-basis mutations with phase provenance; a stronger validated decoder; larger target3 positive/near-frontier pools; a matched historical-policy baseline and equal-compute strategy study; broader cost-aware allocation; true remote backend validation with authorized infrastructure; dependency-version binding in cached task identities; strict typing/format standardization; and owner-confirmed licensing, citation authors and community contact before public publication.
